23. SUBSCRIPTION AND ENTITLEMENT ARCHITECTURE

Do NOT mix these concepts together.

Maintain separate concepts for:

Account

Who the user is.

Subscription

Whether the student has paid for the EduPulse service for the relevant term.

Entitlement

What functionality the student/parent is currently allowed to use.

NFC Card State

Whether the physical NFC card is active, locked, suspended, lost, replaced, etc.

These must be linked but should not be one single database field.

24. ACTIVE SUBSCRIPTION RULE

The source of truth for paid EduPulse access must be:

VERIFIED ACTIVE SUBSCRIPTION

If the student's subscription is not verified and active:

- Student core paid-service access must be restricted.
- NFC card must be locked/inactive.
- Parent access to that child's core paid-service functionality must be restricted.
- The student record itself must NOT be deleted.
- Historical academic records must NOT be deleted.
- Historical attendance must NOT be deleted.
- Historical payment records must NOT be deleted.

When payment becomes successfully verified:

- Activate subscription.
- Activate entitlement.
- Activate/restore NFC card where appropriate.
- Restore permitted student access.
- Restore permitted parent access to that child.
