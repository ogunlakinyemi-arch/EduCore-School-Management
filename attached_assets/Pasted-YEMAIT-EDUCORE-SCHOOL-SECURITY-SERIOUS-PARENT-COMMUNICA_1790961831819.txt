5. NFC ENTRY AND EXIT SECURITY

Use the existing NFC card/device architecture.

When a card is tapped:

1. Read NFC UID.
2. Identify card.
3. Confirm card belongs to the school.
4. Confirm card is active.
5. Confirm person exists.
6. Confirm student/staff status.
7. Resolve current school record.
8. Determine whether the event is entry, exit, attendance, or another authorized access event.
9. Record the event.
10. Trigger appropriate notifications.
11. Update campus presence where applicable.

Do not assume every NFC tap is automatically an attendance event.

Security events and attendance events should be related but logically distinct.

NFC Card
→ Security Event

Do not replace the existing attendance system.
