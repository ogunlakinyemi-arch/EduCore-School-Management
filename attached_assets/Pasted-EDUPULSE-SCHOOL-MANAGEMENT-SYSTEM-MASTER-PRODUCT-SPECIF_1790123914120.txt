MASTER PRODUCT SPECIFICATION FOR REPLIT

You are the primary development agent for the EduPulse School Management System.

This project is being developed in Replit.

There is already an existing EduPulse codebase in this Replit project. It was previously developed from an earlier specification using another AI development environment.

DO NOT automatically delete, reset, or replace the existing application.

Instead, inspect the existing project and reconcile it against this master specification.

Preserve correct existing functionality.

8. PARTNER SCHOOL ONBOARDING

Every partner must have a unique school onboarding link.

Example:

https://edupulse.example/partner/YEMI001/register-school

When a school uses this link:

1. The system identifies the partner.
2. The school registration process begins.
3. The school is created as a new tenant.
4. The school is permanently associated with that partner.
5. The relationship is recorded in the database.
6. The partner's dashboard updates.
7. The Platform Owner can see the relationship.

The partner must NOT be able to manipulate the URL to assign the school to another partner.

The backend must verify partner attribution.

---

10. ATTRIBUTION PROTECTION

The system must prevent partner disputes.

Once a school has been successfully onboarded through a partner's official onboarding link, the system should record that partner as the originating partner.

A partner must not be able to claim an existing school simply by entering its name or school code.

If an existing school is already attributed to another partner:

- A new partner cannot overwrite the relationship.
- Platform Owner may review the case.
- Platform Owner may change the attribution if company policy allows.
- Every manual attribution change must be audited.

Create an attribution history/audit trail.

---

11. PARTNER COMMISSION

Initial partner commission:

₦100 PER ELIGIBLE STUDENT PER TERM

The commission applies to eligible student subscriptions associated with schools attributed to that partner.

Example:

School A has:

100 eligible students.

Partner commission:

100 × ₦100 = ₦10,000 for that term.

If the school has:

250 eligible students:

250 × ₦100 = ₦25,000 for that term.

Partner commission is calculated per term.

There is NO automatic expiration date.

The relationship continues unless:

- Platform Owner deactivates the partner
- School attribution is changed according to company policy
- Company changes the partner program
- Company terminates the partnership
- Other configured business rules apply

Do not implement an automatic partner expiry date.

---

Term 2:
₦125/student

Term 3:
₦125/student

Do not recalculate historical earnings using the new rate.

14. ELIGIBLE STUDENT DEFINITION

Do not calculate partner commission from every student record in the database.

Commission should be based on an explicit eligibility rule.

Initial rule:

A student is eligible for partner commission when their EduPulse subscription for the relevant term has been successfully verified/paid according to the platform subscription rules.

A student whose subscription is:

- Unpaid
- Failed
- Cancelled
- Rejected

must not generate commission.

A student should not generate duplicate commission for the same subscription transaction.

Use unique transaction/reference constraints.

28. PARTNER ATTRIBUTION EDGE CASES

Handle these cases explicitly.

Case 1: School registers through Partner A

Partner A receives attribution.

Case 2: School registers directly

No partner attribution unless Platform Owner manually assigns one.

Case 3: School attempts to register through Partner B after already being attributed to Partner A

Do not overwrite Partner A.

Notify/flag the conflict for Platform Owner review.

Case 4: Platform Owner manually changes attribution

Require appropriate authorization.

Record:

- Previous partner
- New partner
- Reason
- User who changed it
- Date/time

Commission history already generated under the previous partner must not silently disappear.

Use adjustment/reversal records if financial correction is required.

Case 5: Partner is deactivated

Historical commissions remain.

Existing school attribution remains unless Platform Owner explicitly changes it.

No new school referrals should be accepted from the deactivated partner.
