2. REVIEW THE FINANCE REQUIREMENT

The recommended Finance relationship is:

student_subscription_allocations
(student_id, school_id)
->
students
(id, school_id)

ON DELETE RESTRICT
ON UPDATE NO ACTION

The purpose is to ensure that a Finance allocation cannot silently become associated with a different school.

Explain in plain English what this means for:

Example:

Student A belongs to School A.

Student A pays ₦5,000 for Term 1.

The payment generates:

School A: ₦2,000
Platform: ₦3,000

Later Student A transfers to School B.

Determine what should happen to the historical Term 1 Finance records.

The expected accounting principle is:

THE ORIGINAL PAYMENT AND ALLOCATION MUST REMAIN WITH SCHOOL A.

Do not move or rewrite the historical financial record.

6. ACCOUNTING PRINCIPLE

Use this principle as the baseline:

A completed financial transaction belongs to the school and accounting period under which it was originally created.

A later student transfer must not:

- move the payment
- move allocations
- change the original school
- change the original Partner attribution
- change the original commission allocation
- rewrite the receipt
- rewrite historical subscription ownership

Document whether this principle is compatible with the existing schema.
