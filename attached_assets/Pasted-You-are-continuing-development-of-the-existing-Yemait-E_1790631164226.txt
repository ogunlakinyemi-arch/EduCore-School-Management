23. BANK TRANSFER

Support bank transfer in two ways.

MODEL A — AUTOMATIC BANK TRANSFER

Where provider supports virtual accounts/dedicated payment references:

1. Parent selects Bank Transfer.
2. System creates payment attempt.
3. Provider generates dedicated account/reference.
4. Parent transfers money.
5. Provider detects transfer.
6. Provider sends webhook.
7. Yemait EduCore verifies transaction.
8. Payment becomes VERIFIED.
9. Invoice updates automatically.
10. Receipt is generated.

This is the preferred automated bank-transfer model.

MODEL B — MANUAL BANK TRANSFER

Where automatic bank-transfer detection is unavailable:

1. Parent selects Bank Transfer.
2. System displays approved school bank information.
3. Parent transfers money.
4. Parent enters:
   - Amount
   - Bank
   - Transfer reference
   - Date
5. Parent may upload proof of payment.
6. Payment becomes PENDING.
7. Accountant reviews.
8. Accountant verifies or rejects.

Only after verification should the student's balance update.

Clearly distinguish automatic and manual bank transfer.
