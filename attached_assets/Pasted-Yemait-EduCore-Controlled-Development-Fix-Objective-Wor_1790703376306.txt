3. Frontend receives only the information it is supposed to receive.
4. Secret credentials remain server-side.
5. Backend verifies transactions independently.
6. Client-side success must NOT automatically mark a payment VERIFIED.
7. Verified payments update the correct invoice/payment records.
8. Receipt generation only occurs after verified payment.
9. Partial payments continue to work where the existing Finance rules permit them.
10. Duplicate callbacks/webhooks are idempotent.
11. Failed/cancelled payments do not become VERIFIED.
12. Reversed/refunded payments preserve the financial history.
13. Payment records remain school-scoped.
14. A payment belonging to School A cannot affect School B.
15. Parent access remains limited to their linked children/invoices.
16. Accountant/School Admin permissions remain correct.
17. Platform Owner does not become a School Accountant merely because payment APIs exist.

Do NOT enable production payment credentials.

Error handling
