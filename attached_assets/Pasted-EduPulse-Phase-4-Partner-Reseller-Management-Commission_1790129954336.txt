---

1. OBJECTIVE

Build a complete Partner/Reseller ecosystem for EduPulse.

The Partner system is separate from school administration.

A partner introduces schools to EduPulse and earns:

₦100 per eligible student per term

The partner relationship has no automatic expiry.

A school remains attributed to the partner until the Platform Owner changes the attribution according to company policy.

The commission system must be designed so that the rate can be changed in the future without changing historical commission records.

---

2. PARTNER ROLES

Introduce a new role:

PARTNER

Do not give PARTNER school administration privileges.

A partner should NOT automatically become:

- SCHOOL_ADMIN
- TEACHER
- ACCOUNTANT
- STAFF
- PLATFORM_OWNER

Partner access must be limited to the partner ecosystem.

A partner can:

- manage their own partner profile
- view their own referred schools
- invite schools
- view their own referral activity
- view eligible students associated with their referred schools at the permitted aggregate/detail level
- view their commission
- view payout history
- view payout status
- maintain permitted payout information

A partner cannot:

- manage school academic data
- manage school users
- modify student records
- modify subscriptions
- modify commission rates
- modify school attribution
- mark commissions as paid
- access another partner's information
- access another partner's schools
- access unrelated school financial records

---

3. PARTNER STATUS

Support:

- INVITED
- ACTIVE
- SUSPENDED
- DEACTIVATED

Rules:

INVITED

Partner invitation created but registration not completed.

ACTIVE

Partner can access the partner portal and onboard schools.

SUSPENDED

Partner cannot onboard new schools or perform normal partner activities, but historical information remains available to authorized Platform Owners.

DEACTIVATED

Partner cannot access the partner portal or onboard new schools.

Never delete historical partner records merely because a partner becomes inactive.

5. PARTNER INVITATION

The Platform Owner must be able to invite a partner.

Create secure partner invitation functionality.

Platform Owner workflow:

1. Platform Owner selects "Invite Partner"
2. Enters partner information
3. System creates a secure invitation
4. System generates an invitation link
5. Platform Owner can copy/share the link
6. Partner opens the link
7. Partner registers/authenticates using Clerk
8. Partner account becomes associated with the partner record
9. Partner status becomes ACTIVE after successful completion/approval according to the chosen workflow

Do not grant partner privileges merely because someone knows an invitation URL.

Invitation tokens must be:

- cryptographically secure
- difficult to guess
- stored safely
- single-use where appropriate
- auditable

Do not expose sensitive information in the invitation token.

The referral link should:

- identify the partner server-side

- not expose sensitive partner information
- remain active indefinitely unless the Platform Owner changes the policy
- be auditable

Partner should have:

My Referral Link

Invite a School

Method C

- school name

- email

- flag the conflict
- allow Platform Owner review

Platform Owner can decide whether attribution should change according to company policy.

Every attribution change must be audited.

12. COMMISSION RULE

Default commission:

₦100 per eligible student per term

However, do NOT hard-code ₦100 into multiple parts of the application.

Create a configurable commission-rule structure.

Support:

- Commission rule ID
- Rate
- Currency
- Calculation basis
- Effective date
- End date
- Status
- Created by
- Created date

Calculation basis:

PER_ELIGIBLE_STUDENT_PER_TERM

The Platform Owner should eventually be able to change the commission rate.

Historical commission records must retain the exact rate used when they were generated.

Example:

Term 1:
₦100

Future term:
₦125

Term 1 records remain ₦100.

---

13. COMMISSION CALCULATION

Commission must be based on eligible/verified student subscriptions, not merely the number of students registered in a school.

Example:

School A:

300 registered students

280 eligible/verified students

Commission:

280 × ₦100 = ₦28,000

Do not generate commission simply because a student exists in the student table.

Use the existing subscription verification/status architecture.

Only eligible subscription records should contribute to partner commission.

Do not count:

- unpaid
- failed
- cancelled
- rejected
- invalid

subscriptions.

₦3,000

Therefore:

₦2,000

₦2,900

₦5,000

Do not increase the student's subscription price merely because a partner is involved.

Make the allocation configurable so the company can change the commercial model later.

Do not break existing historical subscription records.

Existing subscription records must retain their original allocation.
