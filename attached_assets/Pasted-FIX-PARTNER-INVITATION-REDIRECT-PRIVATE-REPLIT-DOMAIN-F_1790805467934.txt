FIX PARTNER INVITATION REDIRECT — PRIVATE REPLIT DOMAIN FOUND

We have now identified the actual problem with partner invitation links.

The partner's invitation link is being routed through Replit's private development authentication flow instead of the public production EduCore application.

The affected URL contains:

- "replit.com/silent-auth"
- "privateDevDomain=true"
- "/__replshield"
- a destination on a "*.riker.replit.dev" host
- "/accept-invitation"

This causes the invited partner to see:

"Verifying session..."

and then:

"Log in to Replit to access a private app."

A partner must NEVER be required to log into Replit.

OBJECTIVE

Fix the invitation URL generation so that partner invitations always point to the publicly accessible production EduCore application.

The partner invitation should ultimately open:

"PUBLIC_PRODUCTION_URL/accept-invitation?partnerInvitation=..."

and must NOT point to:

- "*.riker.replit.dev"
- "*.replit.dev"
- a Replit workspace URL
- a Replit preview/private-development URL
- "replit.com/silent-auth"
- "/__replshield"

Do NOT change the Clerk authentication architecture unnecessarily.
