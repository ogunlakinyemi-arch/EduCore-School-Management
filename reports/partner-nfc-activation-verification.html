Implementation

  • Added two default-off booleans within existing Partner/profile-staff tables. Referral and existing staff role permissions remain independent.
  • Only a Platform Owner can grant or revoke Partner-wide capability. Changes are transactionally audited with the actual actor, Partner, previous value, new value and time.
  • The primary Partner can explicitly authorize other active staff only while the Owner grant is enabled. Staff, including Partner administrators, cannot authorize themselves or others through the NFC controls. Existing generic staff-role editing rejects extra fields.
  • Every operational endpoint derives Partner identity from the authenticated account, checks active profile/account/staff state and checks authoritative current referral scope. Writes lock authority, referral, school and eligible configured readers before assignment.
  • Referred schools without readers remain listed with a clear unavailable reason. Students and existing card inventory are school-scoped, searchable and bounded by pagination. Manipulated authority/device/card IDs and unknown input fields are rejected.
  • Partner assignment uses an existing available physical card in the selected school's inventory. It produces a locked, prepared card, never an automatic financial or subscription unlock. Lost-card replacement and duplicate/employee/cross-school/terminal-card protections remain enforced.
  • Owner registration with a student, Owner prepared assignment, internal officer assignment and Partner assignment share the lifecycle service. The officer retains its existing active-card lifecycle and selected verified reader context; Partner and Owner preparation retain locked status. UID/student locking serializes duplicates; Owner prepared-row locking avoids acquiring the UID advisory lock in reverse order.
  • Cards remain Card → Student → School. Partners neither select devices nor persist a per-reader relationship. Existing scan matching and device authorization code were not changed.
  • The existing Owner Partner detail page contains state/control/history. Existing Partner desktop/mobile navigation contains NFC Activation only when live access allows it. Access is isolated by signed-in identity and polled every 10 seconds/on focus; the backend blocks revoked authority immediately. Operational content and selections are cleared when access disappears.
  • No new roles, payment systems, Partner records, school records or device/card relationship systems were introduced.
