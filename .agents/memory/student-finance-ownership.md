---
name: Student Finance ownership
description: User-stated accounting boundary for school transfers and historical student Finance.
---

A completed student financial transaction belongs permanently to the original school and accounting period. A later school transfer must not move or rewrite the payment, allocations, original Partner attribution, commission allocation, receipt, or historical subscription ownership.

**Why:** The user explicitly established this accounting principle while reviewing student transfers. Transferring a student must not transfer an earlier school's financial history or revenue.

**How to apply:** Preserve original-school Finance identities in future migration and transfer designs. Treat proposed student/enrollment strategies as recommendations until approved; do not record a source-only review as implementation or authorization to run a migration. Resolve destination login, guardian access, subscriptions and NFC access without silently repointing historical records.
