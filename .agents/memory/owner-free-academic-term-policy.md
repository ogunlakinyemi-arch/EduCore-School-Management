---
name: Owner free academic-term policy
description: Owner-selected, school-calendar free usage grants are non-payment entitlements, separate from launch policy and financial history.
---

The Platform Owner may manually choose any existing school and one saved
academic session/term, grant free EduCore usage with a required reason and
explicit confirmation, or explicitly suspend that free entitlement. Retain
immutable administrator/time/school/term/reason/status evidence.

**Why:** On 2026-11-23 the user reiterated that this is not limited to newly
onboarded schools and is a free academic term, not a fixed-day trial.

**How to apply:** Bind eligibility to the exact school-owned calendar term.
Never inherit the grant into the next term, silently extend it after calendar
changes or infer general school authorization from it.

Free grants are usage entitlements, not paid evidence. Preserve all existing
invoices, receipts, verified payments, ledgers, pricing and BRO history.
Granting creates no payment or commission; suspension does not revoke
independently paid access or supersede the free-launch policy.

**Why:** The user explicitly required financial-history preservation and
independently paid access. The later school-date instruction superseded the
former nationwide January anchor: each saved term now uses its own D..D+6 grace
and D+7 enforcement, while an explicit free grant remains a non-payment entitlement.

**How to apply:** Keep prospective usage/collection eligibility separate from
historical payment finalization and BRO eligibility. Manual school and physical
card/security controls remain independent. A grant is not NFC payment proof.
Any future reinstatement/date-amendment workflow requires explicit policy;
do not implement it by editing retained evidence.
