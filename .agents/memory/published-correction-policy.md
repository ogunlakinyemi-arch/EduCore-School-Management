---
name: Published correction safety
description: Why published school corrections preserve financial history and require academic republication.
---

Published ordinary-fee corrections use a replacement version. Reprice existing invoice IDs only when entirely unpaid and without payment, checkout or adjustment history; protected invoices require financial adjustment/review instead. Never rewrite payments, receipts, allocations or previous schedules. NFC subscriptions and bus-only pricing remain separate.

**Why:** Corrections must fix current balances without erasing financial evidence or creating a second charge. Provider initialization history is protected even before a successful payment because the provider may still complete its original amount.

**How to apply:** Recheck eligibility when publishing, not just when creating the draft. Preserve before/after audit evidence, reject superseded sources, and retain replay protection across uncertain responses.

Published score corrections require renewed review and report-card republication; old approval evidence cannot authorize changed scores.

**Why:** A corrected score is not automatically an approved family-facing academic document.

**How to apply:** Preserve the prior report snapshot, invalidate prior approvals, and verify student and parent reads after republication.
