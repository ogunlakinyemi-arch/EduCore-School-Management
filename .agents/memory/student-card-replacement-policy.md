---
name: Student card replacement policy
description: One-time replacement fee, permanent old-UID revocation and evidence needed for Owner issuance.
---

Student lost/stolen NFC replacements cost exactly NGN 2,000, separately from tuition and the regular NFC subscription. Reuse the existing school invoice/payment/receipt architecture; never introduce a parallel payment balance or let an Owner toggle a request to paid.

**Why:** The user explicitly required a distinct physical replacement charge with persisted successful payment evidence, while preserving the student's academic, family, attendance and subscription identity.

**How to apply:** An unpaid, waived, refunded or unverified charge cannot authorize issuance. Only the Platform Owner chooses a different prepared, same-school physical UID; retain both card histories and the old/new relationship. Reporting loss revokes immediately even if invoice creation subsequently fails. Generic activation, reassignment and legacy replacement paths must not reactivate the lost UID or bypass the payment gate. This fee rule applies to Student replacement, not ordinary provisioning or employee replacement fees.
