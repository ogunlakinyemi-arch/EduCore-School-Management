---
name: Card replacement policy
description: One-time replacement fee, permanent old-UID revocation and evidence needed for Owner issuance.
---

Student, Teacher and Staff lost/stolen NFC replacements cost exactly NGN 2,000, separately from tuition and the regular NFC subscription. Reuse the existing school invoice/payment/receipt architecture; never introduce a parallel payment balance or let an Owner toggle a request to paid. Employee replacements are requested and paid for by the cardholder's School Admin, not the Owner.

**Why:** The user explicitly extended the fixed replacement policy to Teacher and Staff cards, with School Admin request/payment and Owner-only issuance, while preserving every cardholder's identity and history.

**How to apply:** An unpaid, waived, refunded or unverified charge cannot authorize issuance. Only the Platform Owner chooses a different prepared, same-school physical UID; retain both card histories and the old/new relationship. Reporting loss revokes immediately even if invoice creation subsequently fails. Generic activation, reassignment and legacy replacement paths must not reactivate the lost UID or bypass the payment gate. This replacement rule does not change ordinary first-card provisioning.
