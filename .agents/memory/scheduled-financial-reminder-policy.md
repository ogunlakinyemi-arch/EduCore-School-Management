---
name: Scheduled financial reminders
description: Fixed weekly school-fee and NFC reminders, carry-forward debt and disclosure boundaries.
---

Monday and Friday reminders use WAT and are a fixed platform rule, not a
school campaign requiring approval or a per-school reminder switch. Deliver
through the existing configured/consented channels independently; preserve
Owner ceilings, global launch holds, operational pauses and opt-outs.

Guardians see only positive live balances on their own school's published
invoices for currently linked children. Internal student remittance, platform
and BRO amounts are not family/teacher disclosures. School payment or an
uploaded/manual receipt must never become platform entitlement. Internal
student remittance reminders belong to authorized school finance; employee
subscription reminders belong to that employee.

Unpaid ordinary fees remain on their original invoices across school terms,
with original due dates and accounting history. Never duplicate debt into a
new term, apply NFC grace to ordinary fees, reset a balance or silently move
explicit invoice/line allocations. Preserve discounts, waivers, refunds and
reversals through the existing accounting authority.

**Why:** The user explicitly requested this combined business behavior while
prohibiting a second notification/debt engine and preserving financial history.

**How to apply:** Recheck recipients, published charges, current balances and
scheduled-day freshness before external attempts. Preserve durable duplicate
protection, independent channels, bounded retry and ambiguous-send handling.
Implementation permission never authorizes real messages, application database
access, migrations, provider calls, settings changes, deployment or publication.
