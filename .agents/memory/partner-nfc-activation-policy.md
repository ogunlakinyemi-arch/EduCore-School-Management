---
name: Partner NFC activation policy
description: Product boundaries for external Partner card operations and Owner-controlled reader authority.
---

Partner NFC activation requires explicit default-off Platform Owner permission, authoritative referred-school scope and an active Owner-linked school reader. Referring a school alone grants no activation authority; Partner staff authorization must be explicit and capped by the Partner's permission.

**Why:** The user distinguishes external Partner operational assignment from Owner device control and existing internal employee activation scope.

**How to apply:** Reuse existing Partner/staff permissions and the secure card lifecycle; Partners must not select, own or manage devices. Cards inherit the school's reader access, including readers linked later.

Revocation stops new Partner/staff actions but preserves historical assignments and valid cards. Activation authority grants no pricing, payment, allocation, invoice or Flutterwave authority.

**Why:** The user requires preservation of existing cards, financial records and independent NFC subscription/payment behavior.

**How to apply:** Check live authority on every operation and within assignment transactions; do not unlock cards, bypass lifecycle/payment prerequisites or delete history when permission changes.
