Teacher/Staff current-term platform access is free effective 27 October 2026:
no new NGN 2,000 charge/checkout or automatic overdue restriction. Resume only
from each school's actual January 2027 second term, then later eligible terms;
missing or ambiguous calendars stay free. Preserve manual locks and historical
paid/unpaid records. Ordinary authorized platform tasks require no NFC hardware.

**Why:** The user extended the free current-term Student pilot policy explicitly
to Teacher/Staff on 27 October 2026; this supersedes immediate staff collection.

Normal Teacher/Staff subscriptions remain NGN 2,000 per academic term, paid directly
to Yetenia through Flutterwave. New payments have no school share, school
subaccount split, school allocation or BRO entitlement. The student NGN 3,200
policy must not reprice employees.

**Why:** The user explicitly required separate Teacher/Staff pricing and full
platform revenue, superseding the earlier staff school/BRO allocation policy.
They also required preservation of existing records, references and lifecycles.

**How to apply:** Use prospective versioned rules without rewriting historic
allocation snapshots or receipts. Reconcile existing in-flight attempts rather
than issuing replacement charges. Operational restrictions supplement existing
RBAC, never grant new permissions, and must preserve account, renewal, payment
status, help/support and necessary read-only information. A staff restriction
must not become a school-wide lock or block unrelated family/administrative roles.
In a genuinely chargeable term, only verified applicable-term payment restores operations; pending,
failed, expired and unverified claims do not. Implementation is not permission
to alter Production data/secrets, initiate live payments or publish.
