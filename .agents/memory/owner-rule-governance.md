Platform subscription billing changes must be controlled by an active global
Platform Owner through reviewed, effective-dated rules. Intended Student
NGN 3,200 and Teacher/Staff NGN 2,000 values are not, by themselves, permission
to issue a new checkout. Student and staff platform subscription school shares
are zero; staff is wholly platform revenue, and BRO commission is separate.
The school-configurable parent-facing NFC fee is a different transaction.

**Why:** The user explicitly requested rule governance in the existing Owner
dashboard while preserving historical snapshots, staff fresh-checkout blockers
and ordinary school/parent workflows. A rule proposal, old receipt or uploaded
claim must never silently convert a financial obligation or imply receipt of
bank funds.

**How to apply:** Reuse native billing/commission and append-only audit
architecture. Require an explicit change reason, activation preview and
fresh server reconciliation before effective-dated activation. Retire without
rewriting previous payments or silently falling back to an older rule.
Assistance receipts may be independently recorded but are never subscription
payments. A REQUESTED assistance case, including cash/offline help, can initiate
a separate Owner-authorized checkout only through the approved verified checkout
engine; do not fabricate bank evidence as a prerequisite. No credential exposure,
Production SQL or live provider activity follows from UI configuration.

For direct non-split Student LIVE preparation, keep the new-release approval
separate from legacy LIVE split authority. New-obligation approval is not
permission to convert or rebill any legacy paid/unpaid student; require
student/school/term-specific reconciliation evidence and separately reviewed
transition where a legacy obligation exists. A synthetic provider test does
not confirm actual merchant-side non-split settlement.

School Admin bank-transfer assistance must show the Owner's existing verified
official company account, not a school recipient, personal account or invented
fallback. Missing or unverified instructions must explicitly prevent reliance
on them and direct configuration to the Owner only.

**Why:** The user requested official Owner bank instructions from the assistance
action so schools can transfer the Student NGN 3,200-per-term subscription fee,
without treating viewing instructions or submitting evidence as receipt.

**How to apply:** Keep account verification separate from bank-credit verification
and Flutterwave payment verification. Reuse existing company settings and assistance
evidence; never auto-approve an account or payment to make instructions available.

The Owner clarified that Flutterwave merchant settlement is configured directly
in Flutterwave. EduCore's saved company account is for displaying the Owner's
receiving instructions to schools requesting optional subscription bank-transfer
assistance, not for setting up merchant settlement, school splits or subaccounts.

**Why:** Calling this record a provider-settlement setup misrepresented the
business purpose and implied that saving it initiated a verification workflow.

**How to apply:** Explain the NGN 3,200/student/term assistance purpose and
independent receipt confirmation. Keep unverified instructions hidden with
contact-the-Owner guidance; do not infer LIVE readiness, verify an account,
or credit a subscription from this record or from the Owner's report that
Flutterwave settlement has already been configured.

## Definitive subscription and NFC payment rule

Only successful, independently server-verified Flutterwave checkout through an
authorized EduCore link establishes a paid student/term entitlement or NFC
activation eligibility. Bind exact reference/transaction, school, student, term,
NGN 3,200 and idempotent finalization. Flutterwave settles its merchant account;
there are no school splits/subaccounts or automatic transfers.

**Why:** The Owner explicitly clarified that cash and money sent to the company
account do not activate NFC, even when the receipt is independently confirmed.
The Owner can help the student pay through a new legitimate Flutterwave link.

**How to apply:** Keep cash/bank receipts as separately audited assistance only.
Never label them Flutterwave success or use them as entitlement proof. Require
a separate verified checkout for assisted students; preserve hardware,
assignment, security locks, financial history, current free ordinary app access,
and the separately eligible internal NGN 150 BRO policy.
