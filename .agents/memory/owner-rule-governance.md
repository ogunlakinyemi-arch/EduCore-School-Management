name: Owner subscription rule governance
description: Intended platform prices, explicit effective-date activation and independent assistance limits.
---

Platform subscription billing changes must be controlled by an active global
Platform Owner through reviewed, effective-dated rules. Intended Student
NGN 3,200 and Teacher/Staff NGN 2,000 values are not, by themselves, permission
to issue a new checkout. Student and staff platform subscription school shares
are zero; staff is wholly platform revenue, and BRO commission is separate.
The school-configurable parent-facing NFC fee is a different transaction.

**Why:** The user explicitly requested rule governance in the existing Owner
dashboard while preserving historical snapshots, staff fresh-checkout blockers
and ordinary school/parent workflows. A rule proposal, old receipt or uploaded
claim must never silently convert a financial obligation or imply receipt of
bank funds.

**How to apply:** Reuse native billing/commission and append-only audit
architecture. Require an explicit change reason, activation preview and
fresh server reconciliation before effective-dated activation. Retire without
rewriting previous payments or silently falling back to an older rule.
Assistance may advance beyond a request only after the Owner has independently
verified a matching bank credit; do not initiate a provider transaction until
the appropriate verified checkout engine exists. No credential exposure,
Production SQL or live provider activity follows from UI configuration.

For direct non-split Student LIVE preparation, keep the new-release approval
separate from legacy LIVE split authority. New-obligation approval is not
permission to convert or rebill any legacy paid/unpaid student; require
student/school/term-specific reconciliation evidence and separately reviewed
transition where a legacy obligation exists. A synthetic provider test does
not confirm actual merchant-side non-split settlement.
