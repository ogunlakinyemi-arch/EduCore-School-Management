---
name: Automatic academic calendar lifecycle
description: School-local lifecycle rollover without changing dates, financial approvals, placements or entitlements.
---
Use each school's own saved dates and Africa/Lagos day boundaries. End dates are
inclusive. Calendar automation may update status/current markers, never invent
dates, promote students, grant paid access or initiate charges.

**Why:** The user requested automatic opening across first, second, third and
future terms/sessions, including missed-run catch-up, while retaining independent
financial authorization and explicitly prohibiting changes to real saved dates.

**How to apply:** Revalidate fresh school-local calendar rows under the same
school calendar lock used by editors. Catch up directly to the period containing
today; do not simulate skipped term openings or replay entitlements. Close ended
periods as INACTIVE. A saved session can remain current during holidays with no
current term. Do not resurrect an explicitly INACTIVE due period.

Automatic activation requires a complete, ordered three-term due session with
recognized first/second/third ordinals. Unknown names and ambiguous calendars
block rather than infer missing terms. An incomplete future session is allowed
while being configured but must be complete when it becomes due.

**Why:** Dates alone cannot distinguish a deliberately complete calendar from
one where a term was never saved; the domain's three-term model supplies the
completeness requirement. If other school calendar models are requested, obtain
an explicit completeness policy rather than weakening this guard implicitly.

**How to apply:** Preserve edited source dates and evidence history. Lifecycle-only
PLANNED-to-ACTIVE/current changes are distinct from material edits. Keep calendar
transition/audit retention atomic and immutable; financial checkout must still
check actual dates, reviewed evidence, eligibility, rules, refunds and grants.
