---
name: Bulk onboarding passport photos
description: Schools bring existing rosters; private photo completion must remain separate from ordinary record import.
---

Schools already have student, teacher and staff directories when adopting
EduCore. Their existing/imported people must be able to receive passport photos
after onboarding, without re-creating identities or editing academic data.
Missing photos must not block ordinary imports.

**Why:** The user explicitly identified existing-school onboarding as the use
case; assuming spreadsheets contain photos leaves imported people unable to
complete Owner-produced NFC cards.

**How to apply:** Keep post-import per-person private upload/replace accessible
from directories. Do not import arbitrary public photo URLs or make passports
public to simplify printing. Preserve School Admin photo management versus
Owner-only official NFC production and existing lifecycle controls.
