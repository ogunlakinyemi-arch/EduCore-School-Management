name: BRO field operations boundary
description: Individual lead ownership and Owner-approved transfers without changing school referral provenance or finances.
---

BRO lead/visit ownership belongs to the individual BRO user, not the whole team.
Only the Platform Owner can approve or reject reassignment/release, with a reason;
inactivity never releases a lead. Preserve original ownership and immutable
visit, claim-alias and transfer evidence.

**Why:** The user explicitly requested a small Development-only field-operations
MVP with exclusive ownership and Owner oversight, without expanding finances,
outreach or automation.

**How to apply:** Keep field-operation ownership independent of permanent
school-registration/referral provenance and commission entitlement. Adoption
links an existing school; it never creates a second school or transfers
financial attribution. Do not add scheduled reminders, paid dependencies,
attachments, Production rollout or outreach as implicit follow-ups.
