BRO lead/visit ownership belongs to the individual BRO user, not the whole team.
Only the Platform Owner can approve or reject reassignment/release, with a reason;
inactivity never releases a lead. Preserve original ownership and immutable
visit, claim-alias and transfer evidence.

**Why:** The user initially requested a small Development-only field-operations
MVP, then explicitly authorized a Field Operations-only Production release on
2026-11-09, conditional on verified serving-primary identity, current recovery
evidence and idempotent ownership/history protections.

**How to apply:** Keep field-operation ownership independent of permanent
school-registration/referral provenance and commission entitlement. Adoption
links an existing school; it never creates a second school or transfers
financial attribution. Do not add scheduled reminders, paid dependencies,
attachments or outreach. Production permission applies only to Field Operations:
never bundle teacher Telegram schemas/features, teacher period notifications,
Owner notification controls or BRO commission actions into that release.
Missing primary/recovery evidence or an unrelated Publish diff blocks release;
preparing reviewed code/SQL is not evidence of Production execution or deployment.
