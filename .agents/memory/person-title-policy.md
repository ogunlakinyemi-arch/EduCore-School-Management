---
name: Separate person honorifics
description: Keep optional honorific metadata separate from identity names and preserve school-operation authority.
---

Honorifics are optional metadata, not part of the saved person's name. Do not
automatically split or backfill titles from legacy names, or rewrite historical
academic/financial snapshots when a title changes.

**Why:** The title request must coexist with preservation of existing identity
and history. Prefixing the stored name would change its meaning and could
duplicate prefixes in existing records.

**How to apply:** Extend other entry/profile/import surfaces with explicit
title metadata rather than name transformations. Title entry does not grant
Platform Owner permission to mutate ordinary school records; retain the
existing Owner read-only boundary.
