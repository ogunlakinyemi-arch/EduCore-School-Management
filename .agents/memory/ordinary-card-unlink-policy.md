---
name: Ordinary card unlink is not replacement
description: Owner-approved return, correction and reassignment must not masquerade as lost-card replacement.
---

Platform Owners may explicitly detach a currently linked Student, Teacher or
Staff card for ordinary reassignment, return or correction without reporting
loss/damage. Require confirmation and a reason; retain inventory and history.
This does not authorize free replacement issuance or resurrection of lost UIDs.

**Why:** The user explicitly rejected requiring a loss/damage report just to
unlink a card from an existing person.

**How to apply:** Keep ordinary unlink separate from paid replacement. Subsequent
same-UID assignment still obeys school, person and activation safeguards.
Preserve event-time person identity: a physical employee card can be reused, so
its mutable current binding must never be used to infer the owner of old events.
Use retained per-person history/event snapshots for historical attribution.
