---
name: Advance subscription authorization
description: Owner approval before term opening permits later checkout, never automatic charging or early access.
---
Future-term financial authorization must be separate from ordinary seven-day
current-term approval. A reviewed term-wide window can permit normal
authenticated checkout only while that school's saved term is actually open,
current and active. It never authorizes automatic charges or operational LIVE
configuration changes.

**Why:** The user explicitly requested unattended opening-day collection
availability without requiring the Owner to approve each school on opening day.
This is not approval to charge students automatically or rewrite saved calendar
dates. Separately authorized automatic rollover may update lifecycle markers
from the school's saved dates, without creating or extending financial approval.

**How to apply:** Keep exact reviewed school/calendar, roster/reconciliation,
pricing and merchant evidence bound. Permit only the expected lifecycle
transition; material changes require fresh review. Retain expiry, withdrawal
and supersession history. Do not fall back to an older authorization after a
withdrawal. Keep the NGN 3,200 fee and separately funded eligible NGN 150 BRO
liability, grants, January-2027 anchor and independent payment verification.
