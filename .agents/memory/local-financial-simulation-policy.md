---
name: Local financial simulation policy
description: Fictional split demonstrations must stay separate from operational onboarding and payment authority.
---

Local fictional split simulation is a demonstration, never an operational school recipient or permission to pay. Acknowledging fiction is not an ownership attestation. Never convert a simulated review or switch into provider verification, operational enablement, allocation confirmation or settlement evidence.

**Why:** The user authorized fictional information only in a separate local-only mode that cannot reach providers or affect existing school, subscription, payment history or settings. That authorization does not extend to genuine TEST onboarding or transactions.

**How to apply:** Keep simulation stateless and segregated from real identifiers and financial workflows. Any genuine recipient must be separately onboarded with legitimate details and normal Owner verification. See `docs/local-flutterwave-split-simulation.md` for the contract and acceptance boundary.
