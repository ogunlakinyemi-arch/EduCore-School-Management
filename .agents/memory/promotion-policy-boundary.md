---
name: Approved promotion policy boundary
description: Published third-term evidence is necessary but not a complete school-approved promotion policy.
---

Do not infer approval for promotion from the existing score/attendance
heuristic, grading bands or the presence of some published results. Automatic
eligibility needs explicit school-approved pass criteria and required-subject
completeness rules. Use only published third-term evidence in the matching
school/session/placement; validation previews cannot authorize execution.

**Why:** The user explicitly instructed not to invent academic rules and to
stop at a safe preview when policy is unresolved. Existing heuristics are not
evidence that the school approved a promotion policy.

**How to apply:** Before extending execution, obtain or identify that approval
and revalidate fresh evidence at the write boundary. Preserve class/subject/
teacher assignments and historical results. Do not represent a read-only
preview as a safety gate on the older batch execution endpoints.
