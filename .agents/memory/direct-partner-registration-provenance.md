---
name: Direct Partner registration provenance
description: Product intent for permanent original-Partner association and compatibility with historical referral workflows.
---

A school directly registered by a Partner must remain associated with that original Partner through invitation sends, replacements, recovery, acceptance, and later attribution-conflict resolution. Retaining an inactive history row while replacing the current association does not meet this requirement.

**Why:** The user replaced unreliable Partner-generated referral links with direct school registration and explicitly required permanent original-Partner association without disturbing existing Owner or historical referral workflows.

**How to apply:** Protect direct-registration provenance even in Owner conflict-resolution paths, while retaining existing attribution handling for legacy sources. Do not silently reassign an existing school when duplicate registration is detected. Historical referral records must remain readable and must not be deleted as part of retiring the referral-generation UI.
