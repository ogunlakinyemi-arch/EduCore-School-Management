---
name: Simplified Owner finance interface
description: Practical Owner controls only, without deleting financial capabilities or changing payment rules.
---

Keep Owner finance focused on school management, granting/revoking a school's
free saved academic term, teacher subscription payment status, responding
to payment assistance requests submitted by schools, and existing BRO commission
accrual/withdrawal oversight. Hide redundant release, merchant-evidence, manual
calendar/payment authorization and BRO eligibility-configuration screens, not
actual accrued commission, withdrawal review, backend capabilities or retained
financial history. Keep school/term-specific multi-student subscription operations
reachable for the Owner, School Admin and School Accountant.

The Owner must also retain clearly labelled all-schools Financial Insights:
consolidated verified totals and school breakdowns, with existing date and
academic-period filters. Keep platform revenue and internal insight reports
Owner-only, separate school collections from platform income, and never count
requests, unverified receipts, unpaid obligations or free entitlements as money
received. Preserve original source definitions rather than combining overlapping
historical and current payment reports into an invented grand total.

**Why:** The user requested focused simplification on 2026-11-27, then explicitly
required restoration of missing Owner commission accrual, BRO withdrawal review
and multi-student subscription selection, then explicitly restored all-schools
Financial Insights. Simplification must not remove these
operational workflows or redesign payment execution and financial policy.

**How to apply:** Add no manual Owner approval to ordinary school payments.
Preserve school prices/rules and free-term protections: free terms are
non-billable and earn no BRO commission. Teacher subscriptions stay fixed at
₦2,000, initiated from the Teacher Portal to Yetenia through Flutterwave,
without BRO commission or Owner approval. UI cleanup does not authorize
changing credentials, LIVE configuration, callbacks, webhooks, verification,
payment records or school calendars.

Owner school-assistance listing is read-only oversight, not authorization to
activate the full financial ledger. It must support all schools and school
filters without requiring unrelated financial-writer readiness. Distinguish an
absent request store explicitly from a genuinely empty installed store, and
surface real query errors rather than substituting empty results.

**Why:** The user requested repair of the failing school-request panel while
preserving individual assistance, independent payment verification and existing
financial safeguards, without Production changes or deployment.

**How to apply:** Keep listing authority Owner-only. Do not install financial
storage, relax write guards, infer payment or enable provider operations merely
to make a read-only assistance screen load.
