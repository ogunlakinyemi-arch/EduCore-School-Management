name: Assessment provenance
description: Assessment creation history is different from the teacher's instructional ownership.
---

Historical School Admin-created assessments remain valid teacher-owned records. Determine the teacher's access from instructional ownership, not the assessment's audit originator. Do not rewrite creation history to make an assessment visible.

**Why:** Genuine teacher acceptance exposed admin-created assessments disappearing from the teacher's list even though the teacher was properly assigned and permitted to enter their scores.

**How to apply:** Cover administrator-created, teacher-owned assessments when testing teacher discovery and subject isolation. Preserve the original creator independently of who teaches or submits results.

New Exam/Record result entry belongs to assigned subject teachers, not School Admin. School Admin reviews and publishes consolidated reports; Platform Owner only previews.

**Why:** The user explicitly changed the desired workflow to teacher entry/submission, automatic compilation, School Admin review/publication, and Student/Parent published-result viewing. Admin must not create exams, CA, assessment structures, or subject scores for this workflow.

**How to apply:** Preserve historical provenance while enforcing the new role boundaries in both UI controls and mutation endpoints. Keep exam-question review separate: teachers prepare/submit, School Admin returns/approves and prints, Owner previews only, and Students/Parents have no access to question papers.
